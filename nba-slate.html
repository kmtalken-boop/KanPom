The exception is a NEWS flag (the injury list moved fair 1.5+ points since the line was first seen and the line hasn't followed). Player projections are SKYHOOK's prop model (usual minutes x shrunk per-minute rates, adjusted for pace, the implied team total, the defense by position, missing teammates' usage, back-to-backs and blowout risk). Market % = the no-vig over % at the Kalshi/Novig/book main line; model % = SKYHOOK's over % at that line. Kalshi's prices have beaten the model on props, so treat model-vs-market differences as context, not picks.
