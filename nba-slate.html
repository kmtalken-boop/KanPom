How to read this. Fair lines are SKYHOOK's market-anchored ratings (built from past closing lines with injuries and back-to-backs removed) plus tonight's back-to-backs and who is listed Out/Doubtful. They sit about 1.8 points from the closing spread on average, and the close is still sharper: a gap is a flag, not a bet. The exception is a NEWS flag (the injury list moved fair 1.5+ points since the line was first seen and the line hasn't followed). Player projections are prop engine v3 (volume x skill: shot attempts x career shooting, potential assists x conversion, per-minute rebounding; adjusted for what the opponent allows and for missing teammates on tonight's injury report; the older SKYHOOK model fills in players the engine can't project). Combo columns (PRA, P+R, P+A, R+A) are the engine's combo projections; model % is the engine's own over % (negative binomial with each player's own game-to-game spread). The % under each line is the BLEND (70% market + 30% model): the raw model alone is overconfident (when it said 70-80% those sides won about 65%), so the blend is the honest number. Market % = the no-vig over % at the Kalshi/Novig/book main line; model % = SKYHOOK's over % at that line. Kalshi's prices have beaten the model on props, so treat model-vs-market differences as context, not picks.

What-if toggles. Every player has a PLAYS / SITS button. It recomputes the fair spread (value above replacement x usual minutes: the same availability term SKYHOOK's fair line uses), the win / cover %, and every teammate's projection and over %: when a regular (20+ usual minutes) sits, teammates get the context model's missing-usage boost (fitted walk-forward on 2024-25, it beat the plain projection on all six stats in 2025-26), bigger for players at his position. The total doesn't change (injuries move SKYHOOK's fair total too little to measure).
