How to read this. Fair lines are SKYHOOK's market-anchored ratings (built from past closing lines with injuries and back-to-backs removed) plus tonight's back-to-backs and who is listed Out/Doubtful. They sit about 1.8 points from the closing spread on average, and the close is still sharper: a gap is a flag, not a bet. The exception is a NEWS flag (the injury list moved fair 1.5+ points since the line was first seen and the line hasn't followed). Player projections are SKYHOOK's prop model (usual minutes x shrunk per-minute rates, adjusted for pace, the implied team total, the defense by position, missing teammates' usage, back-to-backs and blowout risk). Market % = the no-vig over % at the Kalshi/Novig/book main line; model % = SKYHOOK's over % at that line. Kalshi's prices have beaten the model on props, so treat model-vs-market differences as context, not picks.
Forward record (fair line at its last pre-tip snapshot, 168 settled games): margin error SKYHOOK fair 11.6 vs the board spread 11.23; totals 14.51 vs 14.08; the side fair favored vs the board (gaps of 1+): 58-65 ATS.
Built Wed Nov 11, 10:51 PM CT. Injury statuses from ESPN; prices refresh every 15 minutes.
What-if toggles. Every player has a PLAYS / SITS button. It recomputes the fair spread (value above replacement x usual minutes: the same availability term SKYHOOK's fair line uses), the win / cover %, and every teammate's projection and over %: when a regular (20+ usual minutes) sits, teammates get the context model's missing-usage boost (fitted walk-forward on 2024-25, it beat the plain projection on all six stats in 2025-26), bigger for players at his position. The total doesn't change (injuries move SKYHOOK's fair total too little to measure).
