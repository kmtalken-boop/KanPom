Player projections are prop engine v3 (volume x skill: shot attempts x career shooting, potential assists x conversion, per-minute rebounding; adjusted for what the opponent allows and for missing teammates on tonight's injury report; the older SKYHOOK model fills in players the engine can't project). Combo columns (PRA, P+R, P+A, R+A) are the engine's combo projections; model % is the engine's own over % (negative binomial with each player's own game-to-game spread). The % under each line is the BLEND (70% market + 30% model): the raw model alone is overconfident (when it said 70-80% those sides won about 65%), so the blend is the honest number. Market % = the no-vig over % at the Kalshi/Novig/book main line; model % = SKYHOOK's over % at that line. Kalshi's prices have beaten the model on props, so treat model-vs-market differences as context, not picks.
