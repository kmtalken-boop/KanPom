Gap = model minus the Polymarket mid for the home team, in cents. Buying costs the ask plus Polymarket's taker fee of 0.05·p·(1−p) per share (about 1.2¢ at 50¢), so small gaps are noise. A lean needs at least +% expected value after that fee.

Reading the lineups

wOBA is each hitter's season weighted on-base average (Statcast, with his current team). Bars show his run value per game over a replacement-level bat in that batting slot (leadoff bats most often); right of center is above replacement. Hitters under 15 plate appearances are grey italics and count at replacement level. Values are regressed toward the mean for small samples (150 PA of prior).
If sits is the change in the team's win probability, in points, if that hitter sat and the team's most-used available bench bat (50+ PA, on the active roster, not already out) took his spot. Rule of thumb: 10 runs is about 1 win, so a run in one game is about 10 points. Replacement level is league-average wOBA minus .040. These are conventions, not model outputs.
Starters show last season's strikeout, walk and home-run rates per nine (Statcast, 86+ batters faced) and the runs per game he saves against a league-average starter over about 5½ innings. The model's starter inputs use the same prior-season line.
Bullpen is relief pitches thrown in the previous three days against the league's typical load; “taxed” means over 20% above typical.
