Where this stands. The lab makes the models measurably more accurate against real results, but accuracy is not edge: the margin champion shows no demonstrated edge over the closing line on the sealed 2023-25 games (slope +0.07, 95% interval -0.00 to +0.15); the total champion shows a positive reading worth watching but not yet trusted on the sealed 2023-25 games (slope +0.28, 95% interval +0.12 to +0.43); the kanpom champion shows no demonstrated edge over the closing line on the sealed 2023-25 games (slope +0.03, 95% interval -0.06 to +0.13). Forward paper tournament: 2016 settled bets so far, 0 of 34 rules trusted. Nothing here is betting advice.

The lab proposes small changes to each model (turn a feature on or off, change how strongly it is regularized, how fast old seasons fade) and keeps a change only if it beats the current model on seasons it was not fitted to and on a sealed set of 2023-25 games with real market lines. Error is in points (RMSE = typical miss); the market columns show how far the closing line itself missed on the same games.
