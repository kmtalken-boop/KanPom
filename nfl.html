Opponent-adjusted power ratings

Every real completed game's actual score this season, decomposed into opponent-adjusted Offensive and Defensive ratings for each of the 32 teams — the same iterative adjustment KenPom-style AdjO/AdjD systems use. AdjEM = AdjO − AdjD, in real points/game. AdjEM blends toward last season's final rating early on (discounted by real roster turnover) so one early game can't swing it too far; This Szn is the unblended, this-season-only read, so you can see a fast start before it's fully priced into the blended number. A row tagged manual has a hand-adjustment on top of the model — hover it to see the model's real number and why it was changed.

KanPom NFL is an independent, from-scratch project — not affiliated with KenPom or any sportsbook. Team ratings are built from real completed-game scores, opponent-adjusted the same way KenPom-style AdjO/AdjD systems are, then blended toward last season's final rating early in a new season (discounted by how much of that team's real roster actually returned). Predicted lines for not-yet-played games are reconstructed from those ratings plus a real, measured home-field constant, and shown next to the actual market line. This page is a static snapshot and will not update itself as new games are played or lines move.
