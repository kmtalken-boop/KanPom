KanPom NFL is an independent, from-scratch project — not affiliated with KenPom or any sportsbook. Team ratings are built by reverse-engineering real Vegas closing lines (spread + total) into opponent-adjusted Offensive/Defensive power ratings, iteratively solved the same way KenPom-style AdjO/AdjD systems are, then blended toward last season's final market rating early in a new season. This page is a static snapshot and will not update itself as new games are played or lines move.
