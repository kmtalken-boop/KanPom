Three ways to seed it

How the committee picks

  1. Thirteen voters, seven rounds of ballots. Members list the teams they consider the best, then rank them a few at a time (three teams per round, then four) until 25 are ordered. Members are recused from voting on schools they are tied to.
  2. Results first, then tiebreakers. When teams look close, the protocol points to strength of schedule, head-to-head results, common opponents, conference championships and whether key players were missing. No metric is weighted, and record-quality measures that reward wins over strong teams were added in 2025.
  3. Twelve teams under the 2026 rules. The SEC, Big Ten, Big 12 and ACC champions get automatic bids, plus the top-ranked champion from the American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 and Sun Belt. Notre Dame is in if it ranks in the top 12. The rest are at-large teams in ranking order.
  4. Straight seeding. Seeds follow the ranking, and the top four get byes whether or not they won their league. An automatic qualifier ranked outside the top 12 takes the last seeds. There is no reseeding and no rematch avoidance.

Reverse-engineering it

Conference champions

Simulated with KanPom. Where teams tie for a title-game spot, the tie goes to their record against each other, then KanPom. The real conference tiebreakers check common opponents and schedule strength before any computer ranking, so close Big Ten and SEC races here are approximate.

Read this before quoting it

  • KanPom ratings stay fixed through the simulation. In reality a team that wins rivalry week and a title game gains rating, so favorites are slightly understated.
  • The emulator is fit to one committee ranking (Nov 24). It matches 11 of the committee's top 12, but at this point the committee hasn't yet seen a conference title game, so how much it rewards a title is a guess.
  • Some outlets describe the 2026 Group of Six bid as going to the top-ranked team from those six conferences rather than the top-ranked champion. This bracket uses the champion rule. Boise State is the pick under either reading.
  • The bracket shows a single scenario, every favorite winning, and that exact set of 12 teams comes up in about of simulations. Use the odds table for anything probabilistic.
