How to read this

Execution: fair0 = bought at the no-vig fair price (a Novig-style fill); fair1 = fair price + 1¢; pm_taker = the real Polymarket ask plus Polymarket's taker fee; pm_maker = a limit order resting at the Polymarket bid, counted only if the market later trades through it, cancelled at puck drop if not; nv_taker = the real Novig ask (no fee pre-game; Novig charges only on live in-game fills).
Price is the all-in cost per $1 contract (fees included). Edge = the rule's probability minus that price. To win = profit if the bet wins.
Stake = quarter-Kelly, capped at 2% of the rule's virtual bankroll. CLV (after settlement) = the closing fair probability minus the price paid; positive means the market moved toward the bet.
Before sportsbook lines post, the fair-price rules use the Polymarket midpoint as the fair price. Novig rules (nv_taker) started 2026-10-12.
