Bars show points per game above a replacement-level player (green right, red left); "plays" is plays per game the player is involved in; "if out" is the points per game lost if he sits and the next man up takes his plays. Every card compares the idea lab's current champion models with the market line, then lists each team's offensive skill players, how many points per game each is worth above a replacement-level player, and what is lost if he sits (the next man up takes his plays). A lean is the model clearing a 4% edge over the standard −110 price after shrinking its disagreement with the market to a quarter (the lab measures that only a small share of a gap is real information); it is never called a recommendation until a betting rule has proven itself on forward paper bets (see the lab).

What this can't see: player values exist only for offensive skill players (QB/RB/WR/TE) — defense is team-level; there is no public college football injury feed, so starters are assumed healthy; PPA credits both passer and receiver, so player values are marginal what-ifs, not parts that add up to a team total.
