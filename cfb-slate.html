A lean is the model clearing a 4% edge over the standard −110 price after shrinking its disagreement with the market to a quarter (the lab measures that only a small share of a gap is real information); it is never called a recommendation until a betting rule has proven itself on forward paper bets (see the lab).

What this can't see: player values exist only for offensive skill players (QB/RB/WR/TE) — defense is team-level; there is no public college football injury feed, so starters are assumed healthy; PPA credits both passer and receiver, so player values are marginal what-ifs, not parts that add up to a team total.
