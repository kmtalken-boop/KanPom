KALSHI MARKET-MAKING LEAGUE

Five market-making bots each run a population of quoting strategies on Kalshi's sports markets. Every night each strategy is replayed against the previous day's real Kalshi order books and trades (identical rules for all), scored on expected revenue: how far below fair value it bought (fair = bid/ask midpoint 2-5 min before the start) times its volume. Only days after a strategy was born count. The weakest are culled, the leaders breed, new ideas are invented and must pass a backtest to be born. Paper only: the champion that beats your live bot's rules is shown as a promotion candidate; nothing changes your live settings automatically.

    EVOLUTION LAB

      PROPS LAB

      Player-prop models compete every night on games they have never seen. A projection (recent form; ice time, opponent, home/away for NHL; batting slot, opposing starter, home/away for MLB) is paired with a way of turning it into a probability, and each pair is scored on over/under at the lines venues post. A challenger takes over a stat only if it is better on two separate periods with 95% bootstrap confidence. The champion prices that stat's live props.

      LINE BOARD

      Every bot shops every venue below and takes the cheapest all-in price for each bet: exchange taker fees included (Kalshi 0.07·p·(1−p), Polymarket 0.05·p·(1−p), Novig free pre-game) and each sportsbook's vig included. Sportsbooks via Action Network's public feed; games starting within 40 hours, refreshed hourly. Pinnacle, ProphetX and offshore books aren't available without an account.
