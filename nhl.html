Reading the lineups

Bars show each skater's net 5-on-5 impact in expected goals per 60 minutes (offense added minus defense allowed), from a regression that separates a player from his linemates and opponents. Right of center is above league average.
TOI is the ice time he usually plays. G/gm is his 5-on-5 goals per game above a replacement-level call-up. Power play and finishing are not in these numbers, so stars are understated.
Lineup strength is the ice-time-weighted net impact of the 18 skaters dressing. vs regular compares it with the team's usual lineup; red chips are regulars who are out tonight.
Names in grey italics have no rating yet (rookies, call-ups) and count at replacement level.
