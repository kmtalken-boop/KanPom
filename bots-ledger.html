PAPER TRADING. Nothing here is real money. One trade per bet: when several of a bot's rules or strategies want the same bet (same game, market and side; for props the same player and stat), the bot trades it once, through whichever rule got the best price when it first took the position (lowest price at the same line, else the biggest edge). The other picks stay as each rule's research record and can be shown with the "Show" filter; they never count in the totals. "Original rule squad" bets come from each project's fixed research grid (NHL and CFB price at their own benchmark executions, e.g. fair price or standard −110); "bot-built strategies" shop every venue for the cheapest all-in price, fees included. Price = cost of a $1 payout; model = the probability the bot used. Maker orders that never filled are not listed.
